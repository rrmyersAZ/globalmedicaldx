About Global Medical Diagnostics

A clinical-only mobile diagnostics partner for cardiopulmonary exercise testing.

Clinical access without long waits

Hospital and fixed-lab CPET are far and few between and often means long waits and patient travel. Our mobile clinical model brings diagnostic-grade testing direct to the referring practice and the patient.

  • Supervised clinical CPET with gas exchange, Full PFT's and ECG monitoring
  • Structured reports returned to ordering clinicians in about 3 days
  • Insurance billing or $450 cash-pay
  • Service across Orange County, San Diego, and Phoenix

Our focus

We serve referring clinicians and their patients with clinical-only testing — not consumer sports-performance packages.

Partner clinics host mobile testing days by schedule.

Who we work with

Primary care, surgery and pre-op clinics, multi-specialty groups, cardiology, pulmonology, and cardiac and pulmonary rehab programs that need objective functional data.
