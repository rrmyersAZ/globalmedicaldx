## 1. Executive summary

- **What we do:** Global Medical Diagnostics provides mobile, clinical cardiopulmonary exercise testing (CPET) — supervised testing that measures how the heart, lungs, and muscles work together during exercise.
- **Who we serve:** Referring clinicians and their patients in Los Angeles, Orange County, San Diego, Phoenix, and Tucson (Southern California and Arizona).
- **Operating model:** Mobile service — testing brought to partner clinics, offices, and approved clinical sites.
- **Why it matters:** Hospital and fixed-lab CPET often means long waits and patient travel. A mobile clinical model brings diagnostic-grade testing closer to the referring practice and the patient.
- **Report turnaround:** 3 days to the ordering clinician.
- **Primary goal (90 days):** Build denser referring volume in the five priority metros while holding 3-day report turnaround and clear insurance + $450 cash-pay paths.
- **Year 1 commercial target:** 5,500 tests at $250 average revenue per test → **$1,375,000** revenue.

## 2. Problem

- **Customer pain point:** Unexplained dyspnea, uncertain surgical risk, and incomplete functional workups are hard to resolve with resting tests alone. Referring clinicians need objective cardio-pulmonary data without sending patients through slow hospital queues.
- **Current alternatives and why they fall short:**
  - Resting ECG, echo, PFTs, or stress ECG without gas exchange — incomplete for many dyspnea and functional-capacity questions
  - Hospital-based CPET — longer waits, travel burden, less flexible scheduling for referring offices
  - Fixed specialty labs only — limited geographic reach across a two-state region
- **Cost of the problem:** Delayed diagnosis, inefficient workups, patient friction, and slower pre-op or treatment decisions

---

## 3. Solution

- **Offer in plain language:** Clinical-only mobile CPET with referral intake, on-site supervised testing, insurance or $450 cash-pay options, and structured reports back to the ordering clinician within 3 days.
- **How the service works (step by step):**
  1. Referral / order intake (indication, contraindications, meds, prior testing, insurance or cash-pay path)
  2. Benefits check or cash-pay confirmation ($450), then scheduling at a mobile site in a priority metro
  3. On-site supervised graded exercise test with metabolic gas analysis + ECG monitoring
  4. Data review, interpretation, and structured clinical report
  5. Results returned to the referring provider within 3 days; patient billing follow-through as applicable
- **Expected customer outcome:** Clearer diagnosis or risk stratification, actionable functional metrics (e.g., VO₂ peak, ventilatory thresholds, pattern of limitation), and a documentable next step for care

- **Barriers / risks:** Routing efficiency, site readiness, payer credentialing across markets, capacity to scale toward multi-thousand annual test volumes
