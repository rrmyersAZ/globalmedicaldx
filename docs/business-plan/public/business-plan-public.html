2. The Opportunity

Unexplained shortness of breath, uncertain surgical risk, and incomplete functional workups are difficult to resolve with resting tests alone. Referring clinicians need objective cardio-pulmonary data without long hospital waits or patient travel burden.

Why current options fall short

  • Resting ECG, echo, PFTs, or stress ECG without gas exchange often leave key questions unanswered
  • Hospital-based CPET can mean longer waits and greater travel friction
  • Fixed specialty labs have limited geographic reach across a two-state region

3. Solution

Clinical-only mobile CPET with clear referral intake, on-site supervised testing, insurance or cash-pay options, and structured reports back to the ordering clinician within 3 days.

How it works

  1. Referral / order intake (indication, screening information, insurance or cash-pay path)
  2. Benefits check or cash-pay confirmation ($450), then scheduling at a mobile site
  3. On-site supervised graded exercise test with metabolic gas analysis and ECG monitoring
  4. Data review, interpretation, and structured clinical report
  5. Results returned to the referring provider within 3 days; billing follow-through as applicable

Expected outcome: Clearer diagnosis or risk stratification, actionable functional metrics (such as VO₂ peak, ventilatory thresholds, and pattern of limitation), and a documentable next step for care.

Workflow: Order/referral → clinical screening → insurance or cash-pay clearance → schedule mobile site → on-site test → quality review → interpretation → report to ordering clinician within 3 days → billing follow-through

  • Availability: By scheduled mobile clinic days in priority metros
  • Core systems: Mobile metabolic cart, ergometer and/or treadmill, ECG, SpO₂, scheduling/referral intake, secure report delivery, site emergency response plan
