Patient prep & FAQ

What to expect before, during, and after mobile cardiopulmonary exercise testing.

How to prepare

A few simple steps help the visit run smoothly and keep your results useful for your clinician.

  • Wear comfortable exercise clothing and athletic shoes
  • Do not consume Caffeine 3 hours prior to your test.
  • Follow your referring clinician’s medication guidance
  • Bring your insurance card and ID, or confirm $450 cash-pay before the visit

Frequently asked questions

What happens during the test?

Testing involves progressive exercise with breathing measurements and heart monitoring under clinical supervision.

Who gets my results?

Results go to your ordering clinician. Typical report turnaround is 3 days.

How do I pay?

Insurance billing is available, or you can use the $450 cash-pay option when that path is confirmed before the visit.

Where is testing done?

Global Medical Diagnostics provides mobile clinical CPET at partner clinics and approved clinical sites in Orange County, San Diego, and Phoenix.

How do I schedule?

Your referring clinician’s office typically starts the referral. Patients and clinics can also call (480) 806-9044 or use the contact form.

Questions before your visit?

Call us or ask your referring clinician’s office for appointment details.
