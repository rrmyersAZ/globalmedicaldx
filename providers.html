Referral pathway for providers

Order mobile clinical CPET with a clear intake path, insurance or cash-pay options, and reports in 3 days.

How to refer

Call (480) 806-9044 or email info@globalmedicaldx.com — or use the referral form on the contact page.

  1. Share the clinical order details

    Indication, demographics, insurance info or cash-pay preference, and relevant history.

  2. We schedule a mobile clinic site

    Testing is arranged at a partner clinic in one of our priority metros. Full Day sessions and Half Day sessions are available for testing.

  3. Receive a structured report

    Key physiologic findings with next step recommendations returned to the ordering provider — typically within 3 days.

What you receive

  • Structured CPET report with key physiologic findings along with exercise and nutritional plans and recommendations
  • Clinical-only testing with ECG and gas exchange analysis and full PFT's
  • Insurance billing path or $450 cash-pay
  • Typical turnaround: 3 days

Service metros

  • Orange County
  • San Diego
  • Phoenix

Benefits to Physicians

Practical advantages for referring clinicians who need clear cardio-pulmonary answers for their patients.

  • Improved Patient Care - Test provides a more accurate and comprehensive picture of patients health. Patient is diagnosed immediately. Precise data identifies those who need to be further evaluated by a Specialist.
  • Increased Revenues for Practice - CPET is reimbursed by major health plans. By providing the patient, the diagnosis and the exam room, the Physician is eligible to receive reimbursement for each procedure billed.
  • Increased Patient Convenience - Patient has less anxiety due to familiar setting. Less travel and waiting time for test to be performed.
  • No Expense to Physician - Global Medical provides a qualified licensed Technician. Global Medical provides the diagnostic equipment and supplies necessary for the test. Global Medical pays all expenses related to the CPET.
