Clinical CPET services

When CPET helps, what testing includes, and how results get back to the ordering provider.

Clinical CPET

Supervised cardiopulmonary exercise testing with gas exchange analysis, ECG monitoring, Full PFT's, and a structured clinical report for the ordering provider.

  • Progressive exercise with metabolic gas exchange measurement
  • Continuous ECG monitoring during supervised testing
  • Structured report with key physiologic findings for clinical decision-making

Delivery model

Mobile testing at partner clinics — clinical use only.

We do not market consumer athlete or performance packages as a service line.

Common indications

Clinician-ordered evaluations where resting tests alone may not answer the clinical question. Is it the Heart or the Lung causing the patient complaint?

  • Unexplained dyspnea
  • Functional capacity assessment
  • Pre-operative risk stratification
  • Related clinician-ordered evaluations
  • Annual Physical Exams for patients 50 yrs. old +

What information will the test provide?

  • Is there a restriction / limitation to the heart or lung?
  • Functional Capacity classification ( Level 1 - 4)
  • Surgical risk stratification ( Low, Moderate, High)
  • Heart attack risk ( Mild, Medium, Moderate)
  • Physical fitness level for given age, height, weight, sex
  • Caloric intake and Exercise program customized for each patient
